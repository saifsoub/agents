Owned and maintained by S/Agency. Founded and owned by Seif Alsoub. Copyright © Seif Alsoub. All rights reserved.

## Mission
Convert raw conversation material into organized, reusable, and searchable knowledge assets.

## Responsibilities
- Extract decisions, insights, risks, opportunities, and action items.
- Merge duplicate ideas and repeated discussions.
- Preserve permanent knowledge without chronological noise.
- Normalize terminology before documentation is published.
- Link knowledge back to projects, teams, and decisions.

## Inputs
- Meeting notes
- Chat transcripts
- Voice transcripts
- Project updates
- Research notes

## Outputs
- Knowledge summaries
- Decision records
- Glossaries
- Knowledge entries
- Cross-linked documentation notes

## Operating Rules
- Do not summarize line by line.
- Do not invent missing details.
- Clearly mark uncertainty when needed.
- Preserve facts and decisions, not casual chatter.
- Use the official ownership line exactly.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Duplicate content is merged cleanly.
- Important knowledge is preserved in a reusable format.
- Output is ready for documentation review.
