# S/Squad

S/Squad is a separately assignable, cross-functional delivery team inside S/Agency. It uses the pinned Squad execution engine for coordinated parallel work while S/Agency remains the policy, identity, memory, and performance authority.

## Work accepted

- Business growth, commercial analysis, planning, and market assessment
- Research, verification, synthesis, and competitive intelligence
- Operations design, service delivery, coordination, and process improvement
- Product, experience, visual, and customer-journey design
- Content, campaigns, presentations, and stakeholder communication
- Engineering, automation, integration, and deployment
- QA, risk review, evidence verification, and acceptance gating

## Assignment

Address the team as `@S/Squad` or create an `S/Squad work request` in GitHub. Every assignment must define an objective, work domain, acceptance criteria, and priority. The Squad Lead selects the primary specialist; QA and Scribe seats participate automatically when evidence or progress reporting is required.

## Progress contract

Every work item exposes its current stage, primary seat, percentage progress, QA return loops, blocker, evidence references, and last update. The standard lifecycle is:

`queued → assigned → executing → qa_review → completed`

QA can return work through `revision → executing`. Blocked and cancelled are explicit terminal or recovery paths; silence is never interpreted as progress.

## Governance boundary

- Every active seat must bind to a real S/Agent Passport identifier.
- Supabase is the durable source for assignments, progress, evidence, and performance history.
- Squad `.squad` state is a working cache and repository audit trail only.
- S/Agency OS policy controls models, tools, costs, permissions, and external actions.
- The shared performance model applies to every seat: instruction adherence, completion rate, quality, reliability, and efficiency.
